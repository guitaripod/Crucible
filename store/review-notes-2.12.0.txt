WHAT'S NEW IN 2.12.0 (iPhone, iPad and Apple TV; Trakt Community works in the demo)
- Trakt Community: with Trakt (trakt.tv) connected, every movie, show and episode page shows what Trakt members say about it: Trakt's score and how the votes spread, how many watch it and who is watching now, What People Say (what people loved and didn't), and the comments and reviews people wrote, with their replies, likes and reactions. The person can write a comment or a review, reply, like, react, edit or delete their own, report a comment or a member, and follow or block members. Everything is posted to Trakt under the person's own Trakt account; Crucible keeps nothing of it except an unposted draft on the device.
- To see it in the demo: start the demo, then connect the demo's stand-in for Trakt, which approves its code by itself, so no Trakt account is needed: on iPhone and iPad You > Settings > Integrations > Trakt > Connect; on Apple TV Settings > Integrations > Trakt > Connect. Then open Movies > Halcyon and scroll down past Cast & Crew (on Apple TV move down to the Community panel). A comment opens its thread; See All lists every comment in seven orders; Write a Review opens the composer, which shows Trakt's comment rules the first time. Comments, likes, reactions, follows and reports in the demo go to the built-in stand-in only and reach no one.
- Crucible Pro: free installs see Trakt's score, the top two comments with their threads, and can like, react, write and report. Every comment, What People Say, who is watching now and member pages are Crucible Pro. To see the locks: turn on You > Preview Free Version in the demo and open Halcyon's Community again; Unlock opens the paywall.
- Library Research (Crucible Pro): the companion program Crucible Server (optional, free, open source, run by the user on their own computer) now researches any library Plex has no agent for, using the model and search engine the user runs. Crucible only shows its progress and passes on the owner's choices; nothing of it reaches me. It appears under Settings > Crucible Server only once a Crucible Server 0.6 is paired, so it is not in the demo.
- Also: on iPhone, holding the Library tab switches libraries; grids fill every card with its artwork; Recently Watched and Watch History show items' current titles and posters; Crucible Server is reached away from home without a 30-second wait.

Crucible is a client for the user's own Plex Media Server and has no content of its own. Plex requires a paid Plex Pass or Remote Watch Pass to stream a server's video away from home (https://support.plex.tv/articles/requirements-for-remote-playback-of-personal-media/), so I cannot give App Review a working account and server. The demo runs a fictional Plex server inside the app instead (Guideline 2.1(a)): no account, server or network needed.

START THE DEMO
On the Welcome screen choose "Try the demo", then "Start exploring". The account types and settings below are under the You tab.

ACCOUNT TYPES IN THE DEMO
1. Server owner (the demo starts as "Demo"): every feature unlocked, plus the owner's tools: Manage in a title's long-press menu and on a library's header (Fix Match, Edit Details, poster and background, scans), Stop Stream in Server Activity, Server Dashboard under You.
2. Plex Home member with a PIN: You > Switch Profile > Sam, PIN 1234. Sam has a watch history of their own and no owner tools.
3. Managed (restricted) profile: You > Switch Profile > Kids, no PIN. Only family titles appear. Switch Profile > Demo returns to the owner.
4. Free install and Crucible Pro: the demo starts unlocked. Turn on You > Preview Free Version to see a free install: What to Watch, Write a Recap, every Trakt comment, What People Say and member pages, Statistics ranges past Week, the Statistics library filter, the Year in Review poster and Top Shelf are locked, and the locks open the paywall. A purchase there (sandbox) unlocks them for real; Restore Purchases is on the paywall.

OTHER NOTES
Sign in with Plex shows a code to enter at plex.tv/link; an account with no server is pointed to the demo. Local network access is requested to reach servers on the user's home network; App Transport Security allows arbitrary loads because Plex servers on private addresses use HTTP or self-signed HTTPS. No third-party SDK. The data is an anonymous Midgar ID (iCloud Keychain) for Crucible Pro purchases and, only when the person asks Crucible's AI, what the privacy policy and App Privacy answers describe. What the person writes in Trakt Community goes to their own Trakt account, as the privacy policy says. Crucible uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.
