WHAT'S NEW IN 2.12.0 (iPhone, iPad and Apple TV; replaces 2.11.0 in review; all of it works in the demo except Library Research)
- Trakt Community: with Trakt connected, every movie, show and episode page shows Trakt's score and vote spread, who is watching, What People Say (what people loved and didn't) and the comments and reviews people wrote, with replies, likes and reactions. The person can write, reply, like, react, edit or delete their own, report, and follow or block members. Everything goes to Trakt under the person's own account. In the demo: You > Settings > Integrations > Trakt > Connect (the built-in stand-in approves itself; on Apple TV, Settings > Integrations > Trakt), then Movies > Halcyon and scroll past Cast & Crew. Demo posts reach only the stand-in. Free: the score, the top two comments with their threads, likes, reactions and writing. Crucible Pro: every comment, What People Say, who is watching and member pages.
- Out Now on Your Watchlist: with the demo's Seerr stand-in connected (Settings > Integrations > Seerr > Connect), Home shows Glass Orchard, whose third season is out but not on the server; a tap opens Request. A show's page lists aired seasons and episodes the server lacks, dimmed, ready to request.
- Library Research (Crucible Pro): the companion Crucible Server, run by the user with their own model and search engine, researches libraries Plex has no agent for. It appears under Settings > Crucible Server only once a Crucible Server 0.6 is paired, so it is not in the demo. Nothing of it reaches me.
- Also: holding the iPhone Library tab switches libraries; grids fill every card with its artwork; Recently Watched shows current titles; Crucible Server is reached away from home at once.

Crucible is a client for the user's own Plex Media Server and has no content of its own. Plex requires a Plex Pass or Remote Watch Pass to stream away from home, so I cannot give App Review a working account and server. The demo runs a fictional Plex server inside the app (Guideline 2.1(a)): no account, server or network needed.

START THE DEMO
Welcome screen > "Try the demo" > "Start exploring". Account types and settings are under You.

ACCOUNT TYPES IN THE DEMO
1. Server owner ("Demo"): everything unlocked plus owner tools (Manage in a title's long-press menu and a library's header, Stop Stream in Server Activity, Server Dashboard under You).
2. Plex Home member with a PIN: You > Switch Profile > Sam, PIN 1234; own history, no owner tools.
3. Managed profile: You > Switch Profile > Kids, no PIN; family titles only. Switch Profile > Demo returns.
4. Free install: turn on You > Preview Free Version. What to Watch, Write a Recap, every Trakt comment, What People Say, member pages, Statistics past Week, the library filter, the Year in Review poster and Top Shelf are locked, and the locks open the paywall. A sandbox purchase unlocks them; Restore Purchases is on the paywall.

OTHER NOTES
Local network access reaches servers on the home network; App Transport Security allows arbitrary loads because Plex servers on private addresses use HTTP or self-signed HTTPS. No third-party SDK. Data: an anonymous Midgar ID for purchases and, only when the person asks Crucible's AI, what the privacy policy and App Privacy answers describe; what they write in Trakt Community goes to their own Trakt account. Crucible uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.
