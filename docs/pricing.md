# Crucible Pro pricing

Decided 2026-11-08, when the founder handed pricing over. Effective 2026-12-07, the day the launch price was always
going to end:

| Product | Until 2026-12-07 | From 2026-12-07 | Who keeps the old price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crucible Pro Lifetime (`com.guitaripod.crucible.pro.lifetime`) | $17.99 launch price | **$39.99** (was going to be $24.99) | Everyone who bought it: it never renews |
| Crucible Pro Annual (`com.guitaripod.crucible.pro.yearly`) | $7.99 per year | **$14.99** per year | Every subscriber before the change (preserved price) |

USA is the base territory; every other territory takes Apple's equalized point for the same tier (for example
£39.99 and £14.99, €44.99 lifetime). The annual subscription is not sold in China. No free trial (Midgar D22): the
Pro Week stays the way to try Pro. No monthly plan.

## Why

What Pro holds grew past "a player unlock": unlimited downloads, the full Statistics and Year in Review poster, Top
Shelf, and from 2.9.0 the hub features: Manage Requests for whoever runs Seerr, with Trakt's two-way sync next. The
people it is for run their own Plex server, already pay Plex $69.99 a year to stream remotely in third-party apps,
and buy the tools around it.

What they pay elsewhere, read on the US App Store pages and developer pricing pages on 2026-11-08 (each price checked
twice, by a research pass and an independent verification pass):

| App | What it is | Yearly | Lifetime or one-time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infuse Pro (Firecore) | The player Plex owners compare against | $16.99 ($1.99 monthly) | $99.99 |
| VidHub | Media player with Plex support | $29.99 ($4.99 monthly) | $49.99 |
| Emby Premiere | The other media server | $54 ($4.99 monthly) | $119 |
| Plex Pass | Needed for remote streaming in any third-party app | $69.99 ($6.99 monthly) | lifetime listed, price in flux |
| Plex Remote Watch Pass | Remote streaming only | $29.99 ($2.99 monthly) | none |
| Trakt VIP | The tracker Crucible scrobbles to | $59.99 ($5.99 monthly) | none |
| Letterboxd Pro | Film tracker | $18.99 | none |
| Sequel+ / Hobi Premium | TV trackers | $19.99 | Sequel lifetime offered |
| Helmarr | Sonarr/Radarr/Seerr companion | $3.99 monthly | $34.99 (plus $4.99 per service) |
| SenPlayer Pro | Player, Plex/Emby/Jellyfin | none | $12.99 suite |
| Plezy | Plex client | none | $5.99 paid app |

Market medians (RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026, 2025 data): the most common yearly price is $30 and the
median $34.80, $39.99 in North America; high-priced apps convert downloads to paid at twice the rate of low-priced
ones (2.8% against 1.4% at day 35) and earn about six times the first-year revenue per payer ($62.19 against $10.69).
A $7.99 annual sat in the lowest band, below every comparable product above.

So:
- **$14.99 a year**: below Infuse's $16.99, the price these users already know, and below the trackers' $18.99 to
  $19.99, while doubling what each subscriber brings. Still under half the category median, because the free tier
  (playback, browsing, search, the whole Apple TV app) stays generous: that is what brings the downloads.
- **$39.99 once**: 2.7 years of the subscription, which keeps lifetime the better deal for someone who stays (Midgar
  apps sell lifetime about 2:1 over subscriptions) without making the subscription pointless. Well under Infuse's
  $99.99, VidHub's $49.99 and Emby's $119, a little above Helmarr's $34.99 for one part of what Crucible covers.
- **Not before 2026-12-07**: the landing page promised the $17.99 launch price until that day. Keeping the promise
  also gives a real reason to buy now, and by then 2.9.0 (Trakt and Seerr) should be live, so the new price arrives
  with the features that justify it.

## Watch after the change

Download-to-paid and the lifetime/annual split from `myyty app "Crucible for Plex"` and the subscription report,
for the four weeks after 2026-12-07 against the four weeks before. Before the change (2026-10-13 to 2026-11-07): 83
first downloads (60 on Apple TV), 3 in-app purchase units, 1 paying annual subscriber and 1 in a trial started before
D22. If the conversion falls by more than half, the lifetime price moves to $29.99 first (a schedule change, no
release needed: the paywall reads prices from StoreKit).
