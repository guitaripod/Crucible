Crucible Privacy Policy
Effective October 17, 2026
https://midgarcorp.cc/crucible/privacy

What Crucible is
Crucible is a Plex client for iPhone and Apple TV made by Midgar Oy. It runs on your device and talks to plex.tv, to sign you in and find your servers, and to the Plex Media Server you choose. If you are on a Tailscale network it may also ask the computers on that network which one is your server, and only if you opt in does it talk to api.tailscale.com.

What we collect
Nothing. Midgar Oy runs no server for Crucible, receives none of your data and has no accounts of its own. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.

Your Plex account
You sign in with Plex's own sign-in flow. Your Plex token is stored in the Keychain on your device and sent only to plex.tv and to your own servers. Plex's privacy policy applies to your Plex account.

Your Tailscale token (optional)
If a server cannot be found on your Tailscale network by name, you may give Crucible a Tailscale API access token so it can read your device list. The token is stored in the Keychain on your device, sent only to api.tailscale.com, and can be removed at any time in Settings, Server, Tailscale Search.

Your library and viewing history
Titles, artwork, watch history and the statistics built from it come from your Plex server and stay on your device. A Year in Review poster is drawn on your device and leaves it only if you share it.

The demo
The built-in demo runs a fictional library inside the app. It uses no network and no account, and leaving the demo forgets everything it stored.

Downloads
Offline downloads are saved on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere.

Purchases
The policy that applies is the one at https://midgarcorp.cc/crucible/privacy. In short: Crucible Pro is sold through the App Store and Apple processes the payment. Apple's notifications about Crucible Pro purchases reach Midgar's purchase ledger, and from version 2.4 the app keeps an anonymous Midgar ID (in your iCloud Keychain) and sends the ledger the signed transaction record with it, so Crucible Pro is recognised on all your devices. Version 2.4 contains no RevenueCat software; earlier versions check purchases through RevenueCat under its own privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Diagnostics
A diagnostic log is kept on your device. It leaves your device only if you export and share it yourself.

Children
Crucible is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version appears at the address above with its date. Questions go to https://github.com/guitaripod/Crucible/issues.
